Knowledge for people. Progress for the planet.

People & Planet Thrive is the public-facing platform of People and Planet Thrive Initiative Pvt. Ltd., a private limited company incorporated in Nepal on 12 November 2024.

We are building a space where research, publishing, learning and practical collaboration can come together. Our purpose is to make knowledge more useful: helping people explore important questions, share ideas clearly and develop the skills to put learning into practice.

Why we exist

Useful knowledge needs more than a place to be published. It needs context, open discussion and pathways into decisions. Our launch programme brings scholarly journals, books, practical resources and professional learning into one connected platform.

Our foundation

The company's registered objectives include research across natural sciences and engineering, social sciences and humanities, the environment and disaster management. They also cover planning and strategy, baseline surveys, monitoring and evaluation, related publishing, management consultancy, education, skills development and information technology activities.

These objectives provide a broad foundation. The programmes, publications and services currently available are described on their individual website pages; an objective in our founding documents is not a claim that every activity is already operating.

Our areas of focus

Research and evidence

Our research focus connects questions, methods and practical needs. It draws on the company's objectives in research, surveys, planning, monitoring and evaluation to support better understanding and informed decisions.

Publishing and knowledge sharing

We aim to help knowledge reach the people who can use it. Our publishing direction connects research and ideas with readers through the publications and resources made available on this platform. Each title's page explains its author, subject, format and availability.

Learning and capacity building

Our educational direction centres on practical learning, technical and vocational skills, entrepreneurship and capacity development. Each available programme sets out its audience, intended learning outcomes, delivery arrangements and any participation requirements.

Technology and organisational support

Our registered scope includes computer consultancy, data processing, hosting and management consultancy. We seek to connect these capabilities with the needs of learning, research and organisational work, with the scope of individual services agreed clearly in advance.

Collaboration and social responsibility

Our founding objectives include working with organisations and supporting awareness, information and skills-based activities for the benefit of people and the wider world. We welcome conversations with researchers, authors, educators, practitioners, organisations and communities about suitable collaboration.

How we aim to work

We value clear communication, careful use of evidence, respect for contributors and practical relevance. Our aim is to describe opportunities honestly, distinguish proposed work from completed work and make responsibilities clear before a project begins.

Activities requiring separate professional, sectoral or regulatory permission are subject to the relevant approvals. Company incorporation should not be read as an accreditation or endorsement of a particular programme or publication.
